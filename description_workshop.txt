Adds over 20 unique magic types with unique interactions and attributes!
Multiplayer and cross-mod compatible!
Synergistic combat system using debuffs!
Tons of custom visual effects!
Make scrolls to cast various spells!

To use the mod, aquire a magic. You can do this with the Poseidon's Spirit, an item which you will spawn with.
Magics can enchant your weapons with their element in a synergistic system, and enable the use of spell scrolls.

For feedback/suggestions, questions, and bug reports, please refer to our [url=https://discord.gg/D9NpuGdZsY]Discord[/url].
