Adds tons of elemental magic types and fighting styles with unique interactions and attributes!
Explore tons of new mechanics that change how you play the game!
Multiplayer and cross-mod compatible!
Synergistic combat system using debuffs!
Tons of custom visual effects!
Obtain scrolls to cast various spells and unleash skilled techniques!

For feedback, suggestions, questions, and bug reports, please refer to our [url=https://discord.gg/D9NpuGdZsY]Discord[/url].

Talk to Morden if you need help at any point in progression, he can also explain your synergies.
To use the mod, aquire a magic, relic, or fighting style. You can do this with the Poseidon's Spirit, an item which you will spawn with.
Magics, relics, and fighting styles can enchant your weapons with their element in a synergistic system, and enable the use of scrolls.
Every magic/relic/fighting style has unique gimmicks and effects, learning how to use them will benefit you greatly.
